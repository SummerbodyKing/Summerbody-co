The No-Math Meal Blueprint

A simple plate-building starting point from KING SUMMERBODY. My 180-pound transformation took consistent choices over time, not one magic meal.

1. Start with vegetables

Try filling about half your plate with non-starchy vegetables such as broccoli, spinach, peppers or salad.

2. Include protein

Consider fish, chicken, tofu, beans or other proteins that fit your needs.

3. Add carbohydrates for energy

Rice, potatoes, fruit and whole grains can fit into balanced eating. Portion needs vary.

4. Include healthy fats

Examples include olive oil, nuts or avocado.

5. Reflect, don't punish yourself

Ask: What worked for me? What can I make easier tomorrow? Progress isn't a test of perfection.

Note: This is general educational information, not individualized medical or nutrition advice. It does not guarantee weight loss.
