**What it is:** A weekly content series that ties Terrence's PMP and project management skill directly to his real leadership of the Campbell Family Reunion, where he now serves as President. Posts across Facebook, LinkedIn, and Instagram/Reels.

---

## THE CORE IDEA

Terrence is not just a certified PMP looking for a job. He is LIVING project management, right now, leading a 100-plus member family organization he grew from a handful of people. "Project Family" proves his skill in public, in real time, using the most human proof possible: his own family.

Every post does one of two things, alternating week to week:
1. **PM Lesson** — teach one real project management concept, using a family reunion story as the example.
2. **Behind the Scenes** — document what he's actually doing as President right now: a decision, a win, a setback, a plan.

## FIRST 4 POSTS, READY TO ADAPT

### Week 1, PM Lesson: Stakeholder Management
**Concept:** Stakeholder management is identifying who has interest or influence, and giving each person the right kind of attention.
**Family story:** Every relative has a different reason they show up (some for the food, some for the elders, some for the kids to meet cousins). Managing the reunion means knowing what each branch of the family actually wants, not treating everyone the same.
**Draft line:** "Stakeholder management sounds like a boardroom word. In my family, it just means knowing my aunt wants updates by phone, my cousins want a group chat, and my elders want a real phone call. Same information, different channel, same respect."

### Week 2, Behind the Scenes: Becoming President
**What happened:** He was promoted from Marketing Communications Manager to President of the reunion committee this summer.
**Draft line:** "This summer my family made me President of our reunion committee. I didn't ask for the title, I earned it by showing up for years, fixing what was broken, and building what didn't exist yet. Now the real work starts."

### Week 3, PM Lesson: Change Management
**Concept:** Change management is helping people move from an old way of doing things to a new one, without losing them along the way.
**Family story:** Migrating the committee off chaotic 3-way phone calls onto Zoom, and personally training the older members who had never used it.
**Draft line:** "I didn't just tell my family we were switching to Zoom. I sat with the ones who'd never touched it and walked them through it, one on one, until they were comfortable. That's change management. New tools don't work if you leave people behind."

### Week 4, Behind the Scenes: The Book
**What happened:** Rebuilding the 27-year-dormant keepsake book from 34 pages to 184 pages.
**Draft line:** "We had a family book that hadn't been touched in 27 years. I sat with it and rebuilt it, births, weddings, graduations, the people we lost, recipes, photos. 34 pages became 184. That's not a work project. That's our history, saved."
