## WHAT THE SYSTEM IS

For every job Terrence seriously wants, he builds a dedicated landing page tailored to that specific company and role. Existing examples (slugs already live): `/modemed`, `/caterpillar`, `/caterpillar-cx`, `/hollywood`. The homepage `terrencemichaelscott.me` is the general version.

He uses these pages two ways:
1. Online, sent one-to-one to the hiring manager or recruiter he tracks down on LinkedIn.
2. In person ("hit the streets"), printed as a QR code inside a special-delivery vanilla envelope with the person's name on it, so they scan it and land straight on the tailored page.

---

## THE REPEATABLE FORMULA (every tailored page has these)

1. **A slug that matches the job or company.** Example: applying to Caterpillar CX, slug is `/caterpillar-cx`. Keep it short and obvious.
2. **Company research up top.** Show he studied their business and understands their world.
3. **A named problem.** Identify a real problem or gap in that business that the role would solve.
4. **A 30, 60, 90 day plan.** His vision and approach for the first three months in the role, tied to the problem above.
5. **His tailored resume** for that job (each page carries a slightly different, role-matched resume).
6. **The right images.** Professional, on-brand photos of Terrence, and visuals that fit the company and role. No generic filler.
7. **A clear call to action.** How to contact him and move forward.

## INPUTS NEEDED TO BUILD A NEW ONE

When Terrence asks for a new page, gather (or reuse what you already have):
- The **job title and company name**.

Then produce the page in the same structure, style, and brand as the existing pages, publish it at the new slug, and give him the live link plus a QR code for the physical envelope.
