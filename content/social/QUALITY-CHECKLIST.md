# Content Quality Checklist

**Every piece of content clears this bar BEFORE it publishes.** If any answer is "no," fix it or kill the post.

This is the standard for KING SUMMERBODY / Sweat Department. Feed post, carousel, reel, email, DM, comment reply, whatever. Same bar.

---

## THE 12-POINT PRE-POST CHECK

**Voice + Brand (5 checks):**

- [ ] Zero em-dashes anywhere. (Command+F for the "—" character. Kill each one.)
- [ ] KING and SUMMERBODY are CAPS when they appear together
- [ ] No fake urgency, no "limited time offer," no fake scarcity
- [ ] No Ozempic, no weight-loss surgery, no shakes-instead-of-meals messaging
- [ ] Uses real specifics: real numbers, real dates, real names, real places. If you cannot name a specific, cut the sentence.

**Hook + Structure (3 checks):**

- [ ] First line makes someone stop the scroll. Test: read only the first line out loud. Does it make you want the next?
- [ ] Length matches format. Carousel slide = 12 words max. Feed caption = under 300. Reel hook = under 5 seconds.
- [ ] Ends with ONE clear next action. Not two. Not five.

**Conversion tracking (2 checks):**

- [ ] Every clickable link has a UTM. Format: `?utm_source=[platform]&utm_medium=[format]&utm_campaign=[content-topic]`
- [ ] Every post points to ONE place. Not the site + the Skool + the email + the DM. Pick one destination.

**Ownership (2 checks):**

- [ ] There is a real reason THIS post exists TODAY. Not "content for content's sake."
- [ ] King recorded it, read it, or approved it. Nothing goes out that he has not seen.

## GOOD-TO-HAVES (not required, but level up)

- Front-load the emotional beat before the how-to
- Include a photo of King in the post whenever possible (proof > text)
- If it's a story, tell it in present tense until the resolution
- End with a question that begs a comment ("what would YOU do?")
- Reference a specific person you know in the audience by first name (they will screenshot it)

---

## AUTO-KILL CRITERIA (if any of these are true, do not post)

- The post could have been written by any generic fitness account
- The post references a testimonial or client that has not given permission to be quoted
- The post makes a health claim that requires a medical disclaimer
- The post says "you should" more than once (preachy)
- The post is longer than 5 paragraphs on a caption or 12 slides on a carousel

## UTM CHEAT SHEET

Use these in every clickable link. Consistency = usable data later.

**utm_source values (WHERE the click came from):**
- `ig_feed` — Instagram feed post
- `ig_reel` — Instagram reel
- `ig_story` — Instagram story
- `ig_bio` — Instagram bio link
- `tiktok` — TikTok
- `email` — MailerLite broadcast
- `email_automation` — MailerLite automation (BTC sequence)
- `skool` — Skool community post
- `linkedin` — LinkedIn
- `youtube` — YouTube

**utm_medium values (WHAT format the click was):**
- `feed_post`
- `reel`
- `story`
- `bio`
- `carousel`
- `dm`

**utm_campaign values (WHAT the post was about, kebab-case):**
- `break-the-cycle-launch`
- `next-right-choice-rule`
- `180-lbs-story`
- `mens-mental-health`
- `family-step-challenge`
- (whatever the specific topic is)

**Example full URL:**
`https://sweatdepartment.com/guide?utm_source=ig_reel&utm_medium=reel&utm_campaign=break-the-cycle-launch`

## POSTING VOLUME REALITY CHECK

King said "post 100 times a day." That number will get IG to flag the account as spam and tank the algorithm. Real, healthy, growth-mode volume:

| Channel | Feed / Main | Stories | Comments/DMs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram | 1-3 posts/day (mix of feed + reels) | 5-10 stories/day | reply within 1 hour when possible |
| TikTok | 1-2/day | N/A | reply to comments |
| Skool | 1 post/day + engage daily | N/A | reply to every thread |
| Email | 1 broadcast/week (Tuesdays) | N/A | reply to every reply |

**Total = ~5-15 pieces of content per day across channels.** Sustainable, algorithm-safe, growth-positive. 100/day is not.

If you want to hit a huge posting cadence and can't produce that much original content, the fix is REPURPOSING (turn one PDF into 6 carousels + 5 reels + 10 stories), not spamming.
