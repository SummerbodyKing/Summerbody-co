# HOW TO POST THIS

1. Open Canva (or your design tool). Build each carousel using the copy + visual direction.
2. Feed posts: shoot the photo, write the caption, schedule.
3. Reels: record on your phone. Follow the shot list. Use CapCut or Descript to add on-screen text.
4. Every URL uses its assigned UTM string. Do not strip them.
5. Post at the times noted where noted. Otherwise post when your audience is active (check IG Insights).
6. After 14 days, tell Claude which posts performed and which flopped. We will build Batch 02 based on what worked.
